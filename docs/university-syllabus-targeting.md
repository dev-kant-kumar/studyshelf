# University syllabus targeting research

Research date: 13 October 2026. First discovery and comparison pass, not an exhaustive Indian college directory. Official institution sources only. This document is a planning record, not a claim that Study Shelf covers these programmes.

## Current scope decision

Focus on VBU and colleges under VBU in Jharkhand. Expansion to other universities is deferred. The public /vbu guide links available MCA material, labels BCA and PYQs as planned, and lists six college examples from VBU's January 2026 BCA examination notice. It does not claim current admissions or MCA offerings at those colleges. Home, course, semester and subject metadata now target VBU; generic topic chapters retain their existing titles. The broader research below is retained for later reference.

## First target: VBU and Annada College

The published VBU BCA CBCS document starts from 2018-21. BCA C1004, Introduction to Computer Science, places introductory computing in Unit 1 and networking in Unit 5. Our seven introductory chapters and six networking chapters match explicitly named themes across these units. Multiplexing and switching are not explicitly listed in that BCA outline.

This is 13 reusable chapter themes out of our 15 current chapters, not 13 completed BCA syllabus requirements. Number systems and logic gates still need dedicated material, as do the rest of the paper's units.

Annada College offers BCA, has 2026-2029 admission notices, and hosts a matching VBU BCA document. It is a concrete local college target. Confirm which syllabus its current cohort follows before publishing a college-specific guide.

## Ranchi University and Marwari College, Ranchi

Ranchi's published BCA CBCS curriculum is labelled 2019-22 onwards. Semester 1 includes C/C++ programming and architecture; Semester 3 includes data structures, operating systems and networks; Semester 4 includes DBMS. These subject families overlap VBU's structure but appear in different semesters.

Marwari College lists both BCA and MCA and links the Ranchi BCA document. It is autonomous, so do not assume every university revision applies unchanged. Its syllabus portal also links FYUGP material. The easily found non-CBCS MCA PDF is proposed for 2009-12 and must not be used as the current MCA baseline. A Ranchi two-year MCA syllabus from 2020 onwards was located but could not be fully retrieved in this pass.

## IGNOU

Both BCA_NEW and MCA_NEW are verified programmes. The BCA July 2024 guide puts computer basics in Semester 1, organisation/OS/C/Python in Semester 2, data structures/DBMS in Semester 3, and networks in Semester 4. These are plausible reuse families, not unit-level equivalence.

MCA_NEW starts with algorithms, discrete mathematics, software engineering, professional skills, security and labs. Its networking paper MCS-218 is in Semester 2 and adds modern wireless, IoT and security material. Our introductory networking chapters support foundations but do not complete that paper or MCA Semester 1.

## Content and site architecture

Keep one canonical chapter per concept. University guides should map their actual paper codes, semester, units and assessment requirements to those chapters. Do not duplicate identical chapters for every college.

For each programme record institution, campus/mode, degree, regulation/batch, official source, verification date, paper code, unit topics, matched chapters, missing topics, lab requirements and PYQ availability. Distinguish matching concepts from published notes and verified full coverage.

Suggested future guides: /universities/vbu/mca, /universities/vbu/bca, /universities/ranchi-university/bca and /universities/ignou/bca. These are proposals, not created public routes. A college guide needs distinct value such as a verified local syllabus or exam pattern.
